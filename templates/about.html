About Us

"CNN-Based Multi-Modal AI Detection System" is designed to detect and analyse AI-generated content across images and videos. With the rapid growth of generative AI, distinguishing real content from synthetic media has become increasingly difficult.

This system provides a simple and efficient platform where users can upload images or videos and verify their authenticity. It uses deep learning models to detect AI-generated content, identify the source of generated images, and analyse videos by evaluating individual frames.

The goal of this project is to provide a reliable, fast, and user-friendly solution for analysing digital content and ensuring authenticity in an AI-driven world.
